Research Idea Explorer

Every idea ships with its research problem, the gap it targets, a suggested methodology and the expected outcome. Filter by category, difficulty, type and technology — then bookmark what fits.

How to use this

From idea card to thesis proposal.

1 · Filter ruthlessly

Combine category + difficulty + technology to match your skill level and the tools your department supports.

2 · Save & shortlist

Bookmark 3–5 ideas, then open each detail modal and check whether the research gap genuinely interests you.

3 · Take it to a supervisor

Each idea lists potential supervisors — visit the Supervisor Matching page before you commit.
