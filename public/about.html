About ThesisBridge BD

One centralized hub that carries Bangladeshi undergraduate students from "I need a thesis topic" to "my paper is submitted."

Why this exists

Research guidance is scattered. We bridge it.

The idea problem

Students spend months hunting for a topic across scattered PDFs, seniors' Facebook posts and guesswork. We put structured, gap-driven ideas in one searchable place.

The supervisor problem

Finding the right supervisor depends on rumours and luck. Transparent profiles with research areas, expertise and suggested topics change that.

The publication problem

Most undergraduates finish a thesis and stop — because nobody explained journals, peer review or ethics. The Publication Hub does, step by step.

How it works

Four tools, one journey.

1 · Explore ideas

Filter 20 detailed idea cards by category, difficulty, type and technology. Each card opens into a full brief: problem, gap, methodology, outcome, keywords.

Open the Explorer

2 · Match a supervisor

Combine university, department, area and expertise filters — or answer the four-question demo matcher for a ranked shortlist with match percentages.

Try Matching

3 · Track the roadmap

Eight clickable steps from choosing an area to journal submission, each with recommended actions and resources. Mark steps complete and watch your progress grow.

Open the Roadmap

4 · Publish

Seven publication guides plus an interactive journal checklist that tracks your readiness from research question to submission requirements.

Open the Hub
This is a working full-stack build. All ideas, supervisors, resources and statistics are realistic sample content loaded from a Supabase database. Accounts are managed by Supabase Auth, and bookmarks, roadmap progress and checklist state are stored in Supabase, so they follow you to any device. Supervisor matching is a transparent scoring demo based on selected research interests, not an academic recommendation.
