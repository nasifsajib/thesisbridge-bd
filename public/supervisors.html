Good to know

Before you email a supervisor.

Read two of their papers

Every profile lists selected publications. Mentioning one specifically in your email doubles your reply rate.

Check "accepting students"

Profiles show current availability. Not-accepting faculty may still advise reviews or co-supervise — ask.

Bring a one-page proposal

Gap, question, plan, timeline. Supervisors say yes to preparation far more often than to enthusiasm.
